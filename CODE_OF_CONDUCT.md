# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a respectful, constructive space for contributors and users of every background, identity, and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, kind, evidence-based feedback.
- Discuss ideas and outcomes, not a person's worth or identity.
- Respect privacy, cultural context, and the people represented in examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, publishing private information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

Contact [thisaimentor10@gmail.com](mailto:thisaimentor10@gmail.com). Reports will be handled privately and fairly as practical.
