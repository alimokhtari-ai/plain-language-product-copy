# Examples

## AI feature explanation

**Before**

> Our proprietary intelligence layer synthesizes contextual signals to optimize your next-best action.

**After**

> The tool reviews the information you connect and suggests the next action. Review the suggestion before you apply it.

**Why**

The rewrite names the input, the output, and the user's responsibility. It does not imply that the suggestion is always correct.

## Consent notice

**Before**

> By continuing, you authorize enhanced data processing to improve your experience.

**After**

> Continue to let us use your activity in this product to personalize recommendations. You can change this later in Settings.

**Why**

The reader can understand the data use and where to change the choice. A real product must still verify that this description matches its privacy policy.

## Error message

**Before**

> Authentication validation failed due to an invalid credential state.

**After**

> We could not sign you in. Check your email and password, then try again.

**Why**

The rewrite explains the outcome and the next step without exposing unnecessary security detail.
