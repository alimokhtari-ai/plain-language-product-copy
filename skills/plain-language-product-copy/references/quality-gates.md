# Quality Gates

Use these checks before approving product copy.

## Truth

- Does every claim match verified product behavior?
- Are important limits, eligibility rules, and conditions still visible?
- Did the rewrite avoid certainty where the source is uncertain?

## Clarity

- Can a first-time reader understand the message without internal context?
- Is the key action, outcome, or decision visible early?
- Are technical terms necessary and explained when used?

## Informed choice

- Can the reader understand what will happen next?
- Does the copy make costs, privacy implications, and irreversible actions clear?
- Is the CTA specific about its result?

## Dignity and accessibility

- Does the wording respect the reader and avoid blame or pressure?
- Is the language concise enough to scan on a small screen?
- Does it avoid idioms that make translation or localization harder?
